# NovaMart Warranty Policy

This is fictional policy content for the NovaMart learning project.

- Warranty coverage depends on the product and the applicable warranty terms.
- Do not infer a warranty duration when the retrieved product-specific policy does not state one.
- Ask for the product or order identifier when it is needed to determine applicable coverage.
- A warranty question is not automatically a return or refund approval.
- Escalate unclear, conflicting, or missing warranty information to support for review.
