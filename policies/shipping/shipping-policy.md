# NovaMart Shipping Policy

This is fictional policy content for the NovaMart learning project.

- Order status must be checked against the order record before answering a customer.
- A shipped order is not the same as a delivered order.
- If tracking information is not available in the system, tell the customer that the status cannot currently be confirmed.
- Do not invent delivery dates, carrier names, or tracking numbers.
- Escalate orders with missing or contradictory shipment data to support for review.
